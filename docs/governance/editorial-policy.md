# Editorial policy

## Accuracy and neutrality

Factual correctness takes precedence over completeness, speed, and fitting text
onto one page. The tool creates a summary for deliberation, not an official
resolution. Verify material dates, amounts, locations, positions, and outcomes
against public primary documents from the responsible authority.

Match the exact subject, deciding body, meeting, agenda item, document version,
and supporting passage. A proposal or agenda does not prove adoption. Check
amendments, withdrawals, referrals, subsequent resolutions, and implementation
evidence when relevant. Missing evidence means unknown, not rejected or completed.

Treat recommendations, adopted decisions, and execution as distinct. Preserve
the original proposed wording or explicitly label its summary. Do not erase
contrary evidence to make a brief easier to read.

## Alternatives and finances

Separate facts, possible impacts, and attributed political positions. Apply the
same evidentiary standard to every political group. Compare realistic options,
including retaining the current state when relevant; do not fabricate arguments
to achieve an equal number of advantages and disadvantages.

Unknown costs are not zero. An estimate is not a confirmed invoice or budget
allocation. Requested funding is not approved funding. A budget allocation is
not implementation. Explain periods, one-off versus recurring costs, personnel
needs, and remaining uncertainty. Do not add or subtract amounts with different
scopes or periods without an explicit documented basis.

## Human workflow

1. Identify the exact public proposal and its current version.
2. Prepare a brief using checked primary evidence and precise source locators.
3. Check amendments and the actual committee outcomes.
4. Have a second person review accuracy, neutrality, finances, and readability.
5. Record the reviewed version in the municipality's agreed process outside
   this initial application; use authoritative originals for formal proceedings.
6. Preserve subsequent corrections and the previous version in an appropriate
   recordkeeping process. Local JSON downloads alone are not an audit archive.

Authors with a direct political, personal, or financial interest should not be
the only reviewer. Note relevant conflicts in the review handoff.

The current software does not implement formal approval or an audit trail.
Every output remains a working draft even if all completeness hints disappear.
Never describe structural validation as a source check.

## Public-source boundary

Use public documents only. Do not enter or commit confidential meeting material,
unnecessary personal information, or credentials. Git history is not confidential
storage. Blank or clearly fictional examples are suitable for development.

Summarize and link to originals instead of copying entire documents. A URL alone
does not preserve the underlying evidence. Establish appropriate retention in
the responsible body's process before official use.

## Language and presentation

Use plain German, explain specialist terms, and make uncertainty explicit.
Do not shrink fonts or omit material caveats merely to fit one page. Reduce the
scope or reference attachments. Check projection from the intended viewing
distance. Avoid municipal branding without authorization and identify the
author and document status clearly.
